# Reference visual benchmark

This benchmark records the requested visual and interaction patterns. The current task supplied the benchmark description but not the three binary Word/HTML reference files, so pixel-level screenshots and DOM measurements remain pending until those files are available. Never copy company-specific values from a benchmark into a run.

## Word benchmark

- Formal brokerage/consulting report structure with a restrained white, navy and corporate-blue system.
- Strong numbered heading hierarchy; Chinese body in Song/Source Han Serif and Latin numerals in Times New Roman.
- Narrative and evidence alternate with three-line tables, operating trends, hierarchical organization, radar, KPI scenario calculations, opportunity matrix and real enterprise/product photography.
- Average target: one effective visual every two to three rendered pages; no five-to-six-page text-only sequence.
- Product chapters use image-led 2×2 or 2×3 report matrices with product family, series, model, parameters, application, competitiveness and exact source note.

## Cooperation dashboard benchmark

- Dark navy left navigation and a light analysis canvas.
- Executive overview answers value, rationale and entry point before detail.
- KPI strip, company profile, cooperation dimensions, heatmap/matrix, opportunity analysis, entity gallery and evidence boundary are visible without imitating a SaaS landing page.

## Product intelligence benchmark

- Product hero, search, family filter, sorting, grid, image-contain treatment, detail modal, 100/125/150/200% zoom and comparison of up to four products.
- Product images are verified offline assets. Details show model, family, series, parameters, application, source and Evidence Status.

## Unified HTML acceptance

- One `enterprise_research_dashboard.html` combines management dashboard, report visualizations and product database.
- All CSS, JavaScript, data, SVG and publication images are inline; remote resources are forbidden.
- Required width checks: 360, 768, 1440 and 1920 px.
- Every chart has title, purpose/subtitle, units or explicit non-comparability, source/analysis basis and scenario disclaimer when applicable.
- Word, HTML and PPT consume the same `visual_manifest.json` and `image_publication_manifest.json`; display style may vary but values and analytical meaning may not.
