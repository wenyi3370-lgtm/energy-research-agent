# v0.9 quality contract

## Research stop condition

Research is saturated only after every scoped goal family completes R1 coverage, R2 gap-driven depth and R3 triangulation; the last two batches for each goal add no high-priority discovery (or marginal yield is at most 5%); no critical gap or unexpanded high-priority discovery remains; and critical claims are independently triangulated or carry an approved `PUBLIC_EVIDENCE_GAP` record.

Product coverage is `COMPLETE` only when official catalog centers, categories, series, pagination and detail pages are enumerated, every declared item maps to a verified product, and published model/parameter/image detail is captured. Search snippets discover URLs but cannot become verified evidence.

## Release artifacts

All publishers consume one immutable `FrozenResearchBundle`. The standard output contains `01_evidence`, `02_research_quality`, `03_visual_assets`, `04_word`, `05_excel`, `06_html` and `07_validation`, including `research_quality.json`, `saturation_report.json`, `visual_manifest.json`, all three image manifests, `validation_report.json` and `artifact_consistency_report.json`.

Formal Word is at least 15,000 Chinese characters and 30 rendered pages. It uses only evidence-backed Lieflat data charts whose inputs satisfy the selected catalog template's data contract, with complete figure/image source notes. There is no chart-count or chapter-coverage quota: a chapter without qualifying data uses prose/table treatment. Process, relationship, hierarchy, decision-tree and legacy decorative chart fallbacks are prohibited. Word PNG is a direct Chromium rasterization of the same canonical SVG embedded by HTML; a separately drawn Word chart is a blocking defect.

Formal HTML is the single offline enterprise decision dashboard with the embedded product database. It must pass rendered QA at 360, 768, 1440 and 1920 px; static checks alone are not a visual pass.

## Evidence image contract

Discovery, evidence validation and publication are distinct manifests. Publication requires VERIFIED status, a resolvable local binary, matching SHA-256/MIME/dimensions and an original source page. Deduplicate by exact SHA-256 and perceptual hash. Route images to logo, headquarters, factory, workshop, production line, product, product application, equipment, certificate, project or other using explicit page context.
